Critical Information

The 72-Hour Evidence Window

Forensic evidence degrades rapidly. These steps can make or break a case. Please read before visiting the hospital.

⏳
Time-sensitive: Physical forensic evidence (DNA, toxicology, bruising documentation) is most effectively collected within 72 hours of the incident. The sooner you act, the stronger your case.
  • Place the clothing you were wearing in a breathable paper bag (never plastic — it destroys DNA).
  • If you changed clothes, save the original garments separately.
  • Keep any torn fabric, buttons, or items found at the scene.
  • Do not wash, iron, or fold the clothing.
  • If possible, do not bathe, shower, douche, brush teeth, or wash hands before your medical examination.
  • This preserves trace DNA, saliva, and other biological evidence on your body.
  • If you have already bathed — evidence can still be collected. Do not let this stop you from seeking help.
  • Emergency contraception is most effective within 72 hours (available at the hospital).
  • HIV PEP (Post-Exposure Prophylaxis) must be started within 72 hours to be effective.
  • The hospital can test for and treat other STIs as part of the forensic exam.
  • All medical treatment is free of cost under Supreme Court / statutory mandates.
  • A Sexual Assault Forensic Exam (SAFE) is performed by trained medical professionals.
  • The exam is entirely voluntary — you can stop, pause, or refuse any part at any time.
  • You have the right to request a female doctor and/or chaperone.
  • You have the right to have a support person or advocate present.
  • The exam typically takes 2–4 hours and includes evidence collection, photography of injuries (with consent), and medical treatment.
  • The exam is free — the hospital cannot charge you.
💛

Remember: Even if hours or days have passed, even if you have bathed or changed — evidence can still be found. It is never too late to seek help. Your courage matters more than any timeline.

Cover Your Tracks

Protect your browsing history from others who may use this device.

1

Use Incognito / Private Browsing

Open a new Incognito window (Ctrl+Shift+N in Chrome, Ctrl+Shift+P in Firefox) before visiting this site. No history is saved.

2

Delete Specific History Entries

Press Ctrl+H to open your browser history. Search for this site and delete only those entries — no need to clear everything.

3

Check Shared Account Sync

If your browser is signed into a shared Google or iCloud account, your history may sync to other devices. Consider signing out of the browser before visiting.

4

Clear Autofill Suggestions

Your browser may suggest this website's URL when typing in the address bar. In Chrome, hover over the suggestion and press Shift+Delete to remove it.
